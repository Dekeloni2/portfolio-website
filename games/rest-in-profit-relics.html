Every relic in the pool — permanent, equipped passives you collect and load into a capped Reliquary. Rarity isn't just power; it's how sharp the trade-off is. Commons carry a small cost, Rares a real one, Epics are clean power, and Legendaries redefine a whole build (equip only one).
