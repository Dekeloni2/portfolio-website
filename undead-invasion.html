Undead Invasion

A round-based zombie survival shooter in Unreal Engine, in the style of Call of Duty Zombies. You hold off growing waves of undead, spend points on Perk-a-Cola machines and weapons, and get through scripted wave events. Four playable survivors and one Entity watching over the run carry the story. It's my first serious Unreal project.

Why I'm making it

Call of Duty Zombies is a big part of why I became a game designer. The early games ran on an almost embarrassingly simple loop: survive the wave, spend your points, come back stronger. Over the years the mode got buried under systems. I want to see if I can rebuild that experience in my own design and keep it as tight as it used to be.

My main reference is Black Ops III, so most of my research goes back to what it got right. I'm not trying to out-feature it; I want to understand it well enough to rebuild the feel. Using a genre I know well also means the things I'm learning are the engine and the craft, not the design.

I'm making the sound, the animation, the writing and the wave systems myself instead of using asset packs.

What I'm building
🔊

Sound

Every sound is made from scratch: weapons, the undead, ambience and UI. Sound is how the game tells you about threats, hits and weight.

🎞️

Animation

Animated from real reference, both existing footage and video I record myself, so movement, recoil and reloads feel grounded.

🌊

Waves

How the undead arrive: spawn pacing, numbers and pressure per round. Tuning this keeps each wave tense without becoming unfair.

⚡

Wave events

Scripted rounds and surprises on specific waves, so a run has moments instead of a flat difficulty ramp.

🥤

Perk-a-Colas

Perk machines, like in Zombies. They turn points into power for the rest of the run and give you build choices between waves.

📖

Story

Four playable survivors, each with a backstory and a reason to keep fighting, and one Entity with its own motives watching the run. I'm writing the cast now.
