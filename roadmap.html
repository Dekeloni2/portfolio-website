Roadmap

What I've finished, what I'm building now, and what comes next. I picked each project to practice a design skill I hadn't worked on yet, so the order follows that more than the calendar.

Finished

  1. DekelatroPoker roguelike · C#

    A poker roguelike I built alone in C#: hand scoring, run pacing and a shop economy.

    Goal: finish a whole game on my own.

  2. PizzapocalypseSurvival · Construct · Team of 5

    A top-down survival game from a rapid prototyping course. I made all the art.

    Goal: ship something fast with a team.

  3. Trials of the DungeonCo-op board game · Physical

    A co-op dungeon crawler with tile exploration, class design and a final boss.

    Goal: design systems without a computer doing the math.

  4. Brave the WildernessSurvival · Unity · Mobile

    A mobile survival game where every craft merges two items into one. I designed the progression, economy and a 117-item crafting tree.

    Goal: design a whole economy around one mechanic.

  5. TiltanTaleRPG · MonoGame

    An Undertale-style RPG where you escape Tiltan College and fight your teachers as bosses. Released on itch.io in August 2026.

    Goal: boss design and writing.

In development

  1. VampiroSurvivors-like · Steam · Lead designer

    A Vampire Survivors-style game where your skills are a party of servants. You upgrade them and your character on separate skill trees and fight bosses across levels. It's a team project in my extracurricular program, in development since August 2026 and aimed at a Steam release in mid-2027.

    Goal: lead design on a team and ship on Steam.

  2. Rest in ProfitCollection / auto-battler · Unity · Mobile

    Dig up undead monsters on a Disco Zoo-style grid, then use them to defend your dungeon from heroes. Next is a closed test, then a public Google Play launch.

    Goal: design a mobile economy and launch it publicly.

  3. Dogfight EvolutionHybrid-casual · Unity · Mobile

    A vertical shooter where you steer and the plane fires on its own. Flights are limited by fuel, and the coins you bring back pay for upgrades, from biplanes up to jets.

    Goal: hybrid-casual design for the mobile market.

Next

  1. HexboundDeckbuilder · Hex map · Unity

    You place the map's hex tiles yourself, and the deck you build over the run decides every fight. It grew out of Trials of the Dungeon.

    Goal: spatial and deckbuilding systems, and a step up to 3D.

Backlog

  1. RunicRPGAction RPG · Unity · Paused

    A 2.5D action RPG with six connected systems: runes, crafting, scrolls, NPCs, classes and armor. Paused since August 2026 while other projects come first.

    Goal: make several systems feed each other.

  2. Deep HaulIdle / incremental · Steam

    Send submarines down for scrap. Deeper zones pay more and risk more. Still an idea.

    Goal: ship on Steam and tune a long-term economy.

  3. C++ Sword RPGRPG · C++, no engine

    An RPG about customizing your sword, with a setup phase, card collection and quests. I'd write it in C++ without an engine to learn what engines do under the hood.

    Goal: engineering depth.

  4. Tactical Tower DefenseStrategy · Unity

    Tower defense from the other side: you build the monster waves that attack a fortified tower.

    Goal: encounter design, flipped around.
